what the buttons do
  • mount mod — pins the scranton_lobby park from hostpark and mounts it if missing (also tries scranton_avatar for avi thumbnails). Hidden once the lobby is mounted.
  • enable surfroster — mounts if needed, joins (or creates) the lobby for the current page, and turns on following: every later surf leaves the old lobby and joins (or creates) the new page’s lobby. Stays on across reloads until exit.
  • create — invents the lobby tip; you become OP and appear on the roster.
  • join — publish your pubkey on the roster for this path.
  • leave — remove yourself from this path’s roster.
  • exit — stop following surfs and leave the lobby marked “here” (current).
  • refresh — re-fetch tip and redraw the roster list.
  • kick (on a roster row) — OP only; remove that pubkey.
  • trust / untrust (on a roster or comment row) — local: after you trust a key, scranton_avatar shows their thumbnail and alias. Until then you only see the pubkey. Comments stay visible as pubkey text while untrusted.
  • posts — frames people POSTed onto this page's lobby (e.g. a stage member to place here). Each post kind is read by its affordance, like surfroster_stage_member; a post only counts when its author signed the row.
  • scripts — navi_script posts on this page (park application/navi-script). show / run POSTs the park; trusted authors run on surf. Post: scranton_lobby script post <page> <script park> [label].
  • affordances — frames kept beside the page instead of in its lobby, e.g. comments/<page> or stagedir/<page> (shared stage directions). Anyone can start one; its own policy decides what each write may add (one row signed by you, or deleting your own row).
  • settings — nested trust (keys / identities; untrust lives here) and filter (hide authors or kinds; trusted-only for posts that act), plus the lobby prefs dropdowns.
  • arriving from — a post or direction can say it only applies when you arrive from a certain page; it still shows here, and you can always apply it by hand.
